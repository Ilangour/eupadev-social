From "I want an app for my recipes" to a real app, without writing a line of code. 🍝

How EupaDev works: you describe your need, try a clickable mock-up, then the plan is frozen, no surprise changes. AI agents write the tests and the code, two others review everything, and anything wrong goes back to them. You test a preview, and nothing goes live until a human says yes.

Free and open source, runs on your own machines. Still in pre-release, not production-ready yet: come build it with us. Link in bio ⭐
